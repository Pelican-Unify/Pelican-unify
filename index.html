Introducing Pelican-Unify 1.0

Reason. Imagine. Act.
In One Unified Foundation Model

Pelican-Unify 1.0 closes the reason–imagine–act loop for embodied intelligence. Through a shared representation, it unifies chain-of-thought reasoning, future video imagination, and low-level action generation, enabling robots to understand goals, anticipate consequences, and act with foresight in the real world.
